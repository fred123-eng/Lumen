      How to play

      • Fill the grid so every Across and Down answer fits its clue.
      • Tap a square to select it. Tap it again to switch between Across and Down.
      • Tap the clue bar to switch direction, or the arrows to hop between clues.
      • Stuck? Check or Reveal a letter, word or the whole puzzle.
      • On a keyboard: arrows move, Space switches direction, Tab jumps to the next clue.
      • A brand-new puzzle drops every day at midnight. Solve daily to grow your streak.
